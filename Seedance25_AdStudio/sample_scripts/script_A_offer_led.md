# Script A — Offer / Deal-Led

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 720×1280, ~28 s · single female presenter + Hindi VO ·
dealer-promo style. VO ~0–20.6 s; final ~7 s is the outro card + music only.

Presenter = the new consistent presenter (see `presenter_reference.md`).

---
