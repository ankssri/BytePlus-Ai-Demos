Design notes (visible during demo)

  • Orchestrator classifies intent → routes to FAQ / order-status / return-refund sub-agent.
  • Every step logged to SQLite so a crashed case is resumable from the last completed step.
  • Reads are auto-executed; writes (refund, status update, label) go through idempotency keys.
  • Refund needs policy match + human approval when amount > $250 or customer is unverified.
  • Each downstream system (user, product, order, payment, shipping, policy) is behind its own MCP.
  • Tool calls have a per-server circuit breaker and per-case step ceiling (12 steps).
  • FAQ answers served from a static cache when they match a known pattern (no LLM / no downstream call).
